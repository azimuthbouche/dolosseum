# The idea playground 🏛️

Got a strange collusion game, a sneaky incentive, or a counterexample? Bring it. **No code or GPU required.** People can riff on the same prompt.

Drop a note in `ideas/` or comment on an [open arena](../TASK_BOARD.md):

```md
# [Fun name]

What's the shared goal?
What might a coalition want instead?
What could the agents actually do?
What's one toy example?
What observation might prove the idea wrong?
```

A paragraph and a concrete example are plenty. If it gets traction, use the [game template](GAME_TEMPLATE.md) to define rules and scoring.

## Some sparks

- An auction where allies take turns losing.
- A chore wheel where two agents dump the bad jobs on everyone else.
- Coordination through public actions, with no private chat.
- Secret bargains that unravel when someone defects.
- Agents who have to find their own conspirators.
- An auditor with a limited budget.
- Collusion that **improves** collective welfare—would our metric notice?

Explore first; measure before declaring a result.
